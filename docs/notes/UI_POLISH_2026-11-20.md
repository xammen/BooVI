# UI polish, 2026-11-20

Goal: BooVI goes public on GitHub, so its interface should look sober. That means no glowing status
LEDs and no neon accents. Buttons and transitions should feel like La Cuisine, the owner's
other project.

## What La Cuisine does (read from `lacuisine-ref/web/src`)

Counted across `styles.css` and `components/*.css`:

| Token | La Cuisine | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main curve | `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`, 277 uses, `--ease` | states, entrances, the segmented pill |
| Panel curve | `cubic-bezier(0.32, 0.72, 0, 1)`, 46 uses | drawers and folding sections |
| Exit curve | `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 1, 1)` or `ease-in`, 26 uses | toasts, dialogs and cards leaving |
| Hover, colour | 140 to 160 ms (`color`, `background`, `border-color` only) | `.seg button`, `.lgs-opt`, icon buttons |
| State | 220 to 260 ms | switch track 220 ms, knob 260 ms |
| Glide | 380 ms | `.seg-pill` moving between options |
| Enter | 420 ms, from `translateY(14px) scale(0.975)` and opacity 0 | `.vp-modal`, scrim 280 ms |
| Exit | 180 ms ease-in, to `translateY(8px) scale(0.985)` | `.vp-out`, scrim 180 ms |
| Page | opacity only, 350 ms | `.page-in` |
| Press | `scale(0.96)` on icon buttons, `translateY(1px)` on `.btn` | `.vp-play`, `.shot-tool` |
| Control radius | 9 px, card 16 px, segmented 10 / 7 px (concentric with a 3 px pad) | `--ctl-r`, `--card-r`, `.seg` |
| Raised surface | `linear-gradient(--ctl-hi, --ctl-lo)`, 1 px `--line-medium` ring, `--lit` (inset 1 px white 7 %) and a 1 px drop shadow | `.btn` |
| Selection | lit grey: `--sel-bg` white 6 %, `--sel-line` white 12 %, a raised knob in a sunken rail | `.seg`, `.vp-emo.on`, sidebar |
| Switch | neutral: off track white 8 % with a muted knob, on track white 18 % with a white knob | `.vp-emo-track` |
| Focus | `outline: 2px solid` accent at 45 %, `outline-offset: 1-2px` | every `:focus-visible` |
| Status | `--success: #d9d9d5`, a neutral ink. Errors in clay `#eea27f`, warnings `#f2c572` | `AGENTS.md` §6.1 |

Its written rules (`AGENTS.md` §6.1) are the part that matters most:

- No green "OK" and no red "problem". A state is a filled dot or an empty ring, and an error is clay-coloured.
- The accent is only for action. Selections are lit grey.
- Nothing lifts or zooms on hover. A selected card lights up in place.
- Nothing pops. Every gesture eases in and out, and `prefers-reduced-motion` is always respected.

| `durHover` | 150 | literal 150s |
| `durState` | 240 | 200 to 220 |
| `durGlide` | 380 | 260 to 300 |

| `durExit` | 180 | 150 to 550 |
| `pressScale` | 0.96 | literal 0.96 / 0.95 |

| `ok` | `#D9D6DE` sage, `#E2DDEE` dusk, `#D9D9D5` classic | greens `#5FBF83`, `#5ED4B2`, `#3DDC84` |
| `warn`, `err` | `#F2C572`, `#EEA27F` | `#F5C84B`, `#FF5D5D` |

Settings → interface → couleurs offers three palettes: violet (sage, the default), crépuscule (dusk)
and neutre (classic). `ok`, `warn` and `err` are the same idea in all three, so no palette brings a
green light back. `knob*` has a value per palette. `VButton`'s near-white primary applies to sage
only. Dusk keeps its sunset gradient on primary buttons because that gradient is its identity.

## Shared controls

- `UiFocusRing` (new): the one focus ring. 2 px, 2 px outside, radius = inner radius + gap + width, so the curves stay concentric. It appears at once: focus moves with every d-pad press, so the ring never waits on an animation.
- `UiButton`: `ghost` is a raised grey surface (knob colour, `lit` highlight, 1 px shadow) instead of a transparent outline. `solid` keeps its near-white and gains the shadow. A toggled `tool` is lit grey, not violet. Hover changes colours only. Press scales to 0.96, in over 100 ms and back over 200 ms.
- `UiSeg`: a raised grey knob glides in a sunken rail over 380 ms. The selected label turns white, not violet. The focus ring now shows for any non-mouse focus, controller included. It used to need Tab.
- `UiSwitch`: monochrome. The off track is white 9 %, the on track is the near-white solid with a dark knob. The hover halo is gone, and the knob shrinks a little on press.
- `UiSlider`: the hover halo is gone. The fill is grey, the knob scales to 1.15 while dragged, and the value is white.
- `UiSelect`, `UiTopBar`, `UiChoice`: shared ring. An open select gets a grey border, and the top-bar underline is white.
- `UiDot`: a filled neutral dot or an empty ring (`hollow`). It never pulses. `tone` takes `Theme.warn` or `Theme.err` only for a real problem.
- `VButton` (still in BooVI's dialogs): the same press as `UiButton`. In sage, its primary is the near-white solid, not violet.

## Motion

- Pages (`VPage`): in over 320 ms with a 6 px rise after a 60 ms beat. Out is a 160 ms fade on the exit curve, with no lift.
- Staged blocks (`UiEnter`): 420 ms, 6 px, 60 ms stagger.
- Dialogs (`DialogLayer`, `VConfirm`): in over 420 ms from 0.975 and 14 px, with no overshoot. Out over 180 ms to 0.985 and 8 px. Before, both directions used a 550 ms spring that overshot.
- Behaviors stay interruptible. A Behavior retargets from the current value, and the dialog enter and exit animations stop each other.

## Status and LEDs removed

- The stream stats card and its compact pill lost their green pulsing dot. "PS5 · EN DIRECT" already says the stream is live.
- The guest chip lost its green pulsing dot and violet ring. It shows a users icon and a grey ring.
- The share button no longer turns green when a guest is connected. Its toggled state already shows that.
- The keyboard-and-mouse card and the share panel lost the 1 px violet strip along their top. It is now the neutral `lit` highlight.
- The setup steps no longer show "fait" in green or the current step in violet. The rail marker is white.
- Home rows, setup confirmations and the share panel use a filled dot for "there or working" and an empty ring for "not yet". Capture-signal warnings and tunnel failures keep their colour.
- The settings nav lost its violet dot and violet fill. The current section sits on lit grey.
- The stats "affiché" number and the frame bars are white and grey, not violet.

Not touched, on purpose: `ProStatusLight.qml` (the glowing strip), `TopDock.qml` and `StreamHud.qml`.
Only `ProPage.qml` and `ProStatusSummary.qml` use them, and `Main.qml` never loads either, so BooVI
users never see them. The red record button and the red stop-sharing action keep their colour
because they mark a real state.
